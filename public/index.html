How to fly

Side stick (left thumb): the A320 is fly-by-wire — the stick commands pitch and roll rates. Let go and it holds its attitude, auto-trims and protects against stalls, overspeed and excessive bank. Below 50 ft it enters flare mode: the nose slowly lowers, so you flare by pulling gently at ~30 ft.

Thrust levers (right): IDLE · CL · FLX/MCT · TOGA. Take off with FLX or TOGA, then pull back to CL ("LVR CLB" flashes) so the autothrust manages speed. At touchdown go to IDLE ("RETARD") and press REV.

FCU: speed, heading, altitude and vertical speed; AP1, A/THR, LOC and APPR (ILS). The magenta speed is managed, cyan is selected.

Typical landing: flaps FULL, gear down, ground spoilers ARM, autobrake MED, fly Vapp on the ILS. Follow the green flight-director bars or the PAPI lights (2 white, 2 red).

Keyboard: arrows/WASD stick, Q/E rudder, Shift/Ctrl thrust, 1–4 detents, F/V flaps, G gear, R reverse, X autopilot, B brakes, C camera, P pause.
